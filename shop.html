THIS WEEK’S TYPICAL HIT

“Oga, printer account don change. Pay ₦185,000 today so they no hold the job.”

The real supplier did not send that. The number is new. The person who pays bills is busy. This is the job.

THE HOLES

Not Hollywood. Just expensive.

The invoice that is not from your supplier

A WhatsApp that looks like your printer dealer or landlord. New account number. Pay today. The money does not come back.

The staff phone

One tap on a “GTBank” link, and the POS, the business WhatsApp, or the Gmail is gone. Most shops lose access, not “get hacked by a genius”.

One password, three tools

The same PIN on the bank app, the email, and the admin phone. When one leaks, the week of sales goes with it.

THE OFFER

A checkup. A briefing. A quote in writing.

I am not a bank. I will not “hack-proof” you. I will make the boring things true: the right people, the right approvals, the scams your staff will actually see.

02

Close the obvious holes

03

Thirty minutes with whoever answers the business line. The scams they will see this month, in plain language.
